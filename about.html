about me

who's writing all this

Hi. I'm Robert. I'm 41 and I live alone in an old house at the edge of town. Pay the bills running a line at Depp Fabrication — twelve-hour shifts, been there almost twenty years. It's dull work but it's steady, and steady buys nice things.

What I actually live for is getting out. Weekends I'm on the trails with my Sony camcorder filming whatever the woods give me — hawks, deer, foxes, a pileated woodpecker if I'm lucky. There's a peace in watching something through the lens that doesn't know you're there. Lately I've been going farther out than I probably should.

I'll admit I like my stuff, too. Good watch, big TV, the right gear. Working toward a Rolex Datejust one of these years. A man's allowed to want things.

I put my nature clips up here for anybody who cares to watch them. Fair warning: my camcorder saves in a strange format and you'll need the Archive Viewer to open them.
